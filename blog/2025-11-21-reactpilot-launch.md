We are excited to announce the launch of **ReactPilot**, your new AI-powered copilot for React codebases!

ReactPilot helps you analyze, optimize, and fix your React applications with the power of AST analysis and Generative AI.

## Key Features

- **Deep Analysis**: Find bugs that linters miss.
- **Auto-Fix**: Let AI fix your code for you.
- **Performance Score**: Gamify your optimization process.

Give it a try today!

```bash
npx @reactpilot/cli analyze .
```
